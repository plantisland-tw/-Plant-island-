Section 08 — FAQ

Still have doubts?
We have thought them through.

Can I grow seedlings bought from a garden centre?
Yes, and this is one of the biggest differences between Plant Island and every other hydroponic unit. Any hydroponic-friendly seedling from a flower market or garden centre can go straight in, and the variety is entirely your choice, never limited to a brand's pod range. Not sure which seedlings suit hydroponics? Our LINE support team and our grow handbook explain it in detail, and you can ask us any time.
Is the nutrient solution chemical? Is it safe?
Nothing to worry about. It is produced by a licensed Taiwanese fertiliser manufacturer, and the ingredients are simply the inorganic minerals plants need to grow, the equivalent of topping up minerals in a diet. It also passes multiple SGS toxicity tests and contains added vitamin C.
I have never grown anything. Will it work for me?
Absolutely. Even a primary school child can grow a thriving basin with Plant Island. Lighting, water level and nutrients are all designed for you, so no growing experience is needed. We also provide a detailed grow handbook and real people on LINE at every step, and you can buy ready-made seedling trays to skip germination entirely: drop them in on arrival and harvest in 2 to 3 weeks. The risk is not failing, it is getting hooked.
Is cleaning and maintenance a hassle?
Our basin uses a fully open, seamless design that washes in three minutes, about as easy as doing the dishes. We designed it this way after buying several machines on the market and finding cleaning to be the single biggest frustration.
How is this different from cheap machines online?
The biggest difference is that you can always reach us. A Taiwan-based manufacturer, physical stores, a one-year warranty and real people answering questions. The materials, nutrients and seeds all have verifiable sources, so you are not left stranded after the sale.
How long does it last, and how soon does it pay for itself?
The unit is built to last and holds up for years in normal use. At around US$1.60 a month in electricity, the savings from your first harvests cover the cost within a few months. After that, every month you grow is money saved and food gained.
Does it make a good gift for older parents?
Very much so. Many people over 70 lose their daily rhythm after retiring, and growing vegetables gives them something to watch over and care for each day. Our LINE support team can also help them work through any growing questions.
